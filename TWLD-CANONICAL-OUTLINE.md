## 0. WHAT THE TWO OUTLINES ACTUALLY ARE

You asked which two outlines you're choosing between. There are really only two families:

**Outline A — The Book Bible (the blueprint):**
Prequel 0a/0b + Part One's 18 alternating Jesus/You chapters + Climax 19–24 (Descent / First Meeting / Gladiator's Descent 16–33 / Iron Doors intercut / Fall After Rescue / He Came Again) + Epilogue 25–26. Clean, theological, but empty where your life actually happened.

**Outline B — What you actually lived and drafted (Chapters 11–18 as they exist):**
Chapter 11 The Driveway and the Deep Well (homeless, shed fire, Mr. Dean's driveway) → 12 The Giant and the Sanctuary (Lovetts) → 13 wilderness (two variants, both about Jesus at 30 placed beside your age 17–18) → 14 The Closet and the Covenant (Naomi, pregnancy math, chapel prayer) → 15 The Five-Year Forge and the Kidnapping (Sebastian, 20–25, sheriffs, New York) → 16 The Mercy and the Demon (court deal, needle enters) → 17 The Titanium Bone and the Vacuum (Texas, Gabriel, abandoning Sebastian by phone, whiskey, Xanax) → 18 three variants of Valero / Taco Bell / Christmas raid → stops at the arrest. Raw, true, but with no Jesus-side pairs, no prequel, and nothing after the cell.

**The marriage (this document):** Keep Outline A's *theology and ending* (prequel, three Harrowings, Peter/John 21, desk commission) and keep Outline B's *lived truth* (the Lovetts, Sebastian, Texas, the fall). Do not stack them — Outline B's 11–18 *is* the content Outline A planned for Chapter 21. This outline puts it in the right place and restores the missing Jesus-side mirrors around it.

If something contradicts, it's not that you lied. It's either out of context (e.g., the two Chapter 13s belong at age 30, not age 17) or a hole where testimony is missing (e.g., fire vs. Earl's vs. car order). Those holes are listed in the Fact & Holes Register.

## 3. WHY THIS MARRIES BOTH TRUTHS

- **It keeps the truest lived material where it belongs.** Chapters 11,12,14–18 don't get cut. They move from "extra chapters past 10" to the heart of Movements D–E — exactly where the Bible needed a long fall to prison but had no material.
- **It fixes the duplicate numbering without deleting truth.** The two Chapter 13s are not two chapters about you at 17. They are two attempts at Jesus at 30. Consolidated, they become one Jesus chapter at 30 (#23), paired across from your Texas years — not beside your driveway.
- **It keeps the three-bump sanctification arc you locked.** Age 9 parking lot (first touch, then adversary) → Age 33 cell (second meeting) → After-release fall and third rescue via Peter/John 21. No "fixed forever" ending.
- **It protects the short/quiet Jesus device.** Silent-years Jesus chapters are 1–2 paragraphs, not invented novels. The Thief interlude (2,700w) stays intact but as a labeled imaginative interlude, not as a stealth rewrite of the core rule.
