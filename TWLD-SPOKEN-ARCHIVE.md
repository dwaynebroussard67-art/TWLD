# PART ONE — AGES 15–16
## Out of LTI

I went in kind of skinny. I was a heavy kid when I was small — I'd say I was fluffy.
Then my thirteenth year I grew six inches. Never grew a single inch after that. I've
been six foot tall since I was thirteen. It's like my body stretched up as far as my
little extra weight would allow, and stopped when it ran out of food.

Bridge City was a military-style institute. A lot of push-ups, a lot of exercising.
So when I came home I'd put on man muscle.

And it wasn't just the body. I fought a lot before I went to LTI. But in LTI I went
from being a fighter to being a combatant. Violence took on a different thing for me,
because I knew I was really good at it. I was more of a weapon than a lost boy. Maybe
I was equally both.

LTI scared me straight — but not the way people mean it. The torture we went through
in there got me to the point where I didn't plan on not being a criminal. I planned
on being a lot more careful about my criminal activity.

## The parole officer

When I got out, I was going to do the right thing. Get a job immediately.

My parole officer was sure I was going back, sure I'd have to do juvenile life. He was
just waiting for me to fuck up. He told me I had to get a job or go to school. He
didn't care which. The first time I got arrested I was going back for five years
anyway, so it didn't much matter to him.

And in his defense — my record shows I probably didn't have much of a chance of making
it. If not for a retiring man's last wish, I'd have already been gone for a long time.
So he had every right to believe I was going to fuck it up. I can't hold that against
him now.

## School

I'd already been kicked out of seventh grade. Got caught at school selling marijuana,

When I came back they somehow put the end of my seventh grade year and the beginning

## The cafeteria
